## Prior and data
- Kinase-substrate prior: KSEAapp::KSData (PhosphoSitePlus), 19739 pairs / 387 kinases, **offline**.
- Measured pairs after matching to the 11334 filtered phosphosites: 2068 pairs / 1309 sites.
- Kinases with >=5 measured substrates: 78 (of 78 with any substrate). Median substrates per testable kinase: 10.

## Activity differs between ecotypes?
- Tumours with labels: 97 (E1 47 / E2 50), activity scored as the mean z-score of a kinase's measured substrate sites.
- Kinases at FDR<0.05: **9 of 78**; 5 of those have unchanged kinase abundance (p>0.05).
- Largest effects: CDK7 (d=-1.00, FDR=0.000112, 12 substrates); ROCK1 (d=0.74, FDR=0.0043, 17 substrates); PRKG1 (d=0.74, FDR=0.0043, 16 substrates); DYRK1A (d=-0.73, FDR=0.0043, 6 substrates); CDK4 (d=-0.66, FDR=0.00866, 11 substrates); MAPK7 (d=0.66, FDR=0.00866, 5 substrates).

## Activity versus abundance
- Across the 54 kinases quantified at both levels, activity effect vs abundance difference r = 0.31.
- 5 kinases change in activity without a detectable change in their own protein level.

## Grade
- **E1** — kinase activity (inferred from substrate phosphorylation) separates the ecotypes for a subset of kinases, and some of those are not explained by kinase abundance; single cohort, inferred rather than measured activity, so exploratory (E1) only.

## Limitations
- Activity is **inferred** from substrate phosphorylation, not measured (no kinase-activity assay).
- PhosphoSitePlus substrate coverage is incomplete and biased toward well-studied kinases; median substrates per testable kinase is low.
- Substrate sites are reused across kinases (hierarchical kinase families), so scores are not independent.
- One cohort, 96 tumours with labels; no external phosphoproteome exists in this project to replicate.
- Kinase abundance is mass-spectrometry protein level, which is itself noisy at this depth.
