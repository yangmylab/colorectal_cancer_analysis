## Data
- Phosphosites: 31262 quantified in 97 tumours; 11334 kept at <=50% missing (threshold fixed a priori).
- 3414 genes at site level, 2554 with matched protein abundance; 49 of 50 Hallmark programmes scorable.
- Imputation: kNN (k=10); median r(phospho, protein) per gene = 0.32.

## Does the frozen K=2 axis exist at the phosphoproteome level?
- Ecotype assigned from phospho programme scores: E1 47 / E2 50.
- Agreement with the protein-level call: **87.5%** (ARI 0.56); with mRNA: 76.0%.
- Permutation null (1000 label permutations): ARI 95th percentile 0.03, **p = 0.001**.
- Programme concordance: median r phospho-protein = 0.22, phospho-mRNA = 0.19, protein-mRNA = 0.42.

## Differential phosphorylation
- 3095 sites at FDR<0.05, of which 538 also have |log2FC|>0.5 (of 11334 tested).
- Sensitivity with the mRNA-assigned ecotype: 296 sites at FDR<0.05 & |log2FC|>0.5.
- Phospho/protein discordance screen: 2554 genes, 276 at FDR<0.05.

## Survival
- Not estimable: the ecotype coefficient separated with 8 deaths in 96 tumours (log-rank p = 0.012 is uninformative at this event count).

## Grade
- **E3** — transcriptome, proteome and phosphoproteome converge on the same K=2 partition, matching F06. This is a reproducibility result, not a prognosis result: the ecotype-OS association remains null (F01/F03).

## Limitations
- 59.8% of sites are missing before filtering; heavily modified proteins were averaged per gene to avoid dominance.
- Kinase-activity inference (KSEA/decoupleR substrate scoring) is **not** reported: no offline kinase-substrate
  library is installed and no download was authorised, so activity claims would have been fabricated.
- CPTAC-COAD has 8 deaths, so survival is descriptive; this layer tests reproducibility, not prognosis.
- cBioPortal's datahub ships CPTAC log ratios without per-site confidence; low-abundance sites may be noise.
